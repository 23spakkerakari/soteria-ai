Things to check before a public launch:

- The hero and rafting photographs show identifiable people. The licences cover copyright, not a model release, so using these people to promote a product may need their consent. Replace with commissioned or released photography for a real launch.
- A third-party brand name is legible on the backpack in the hero photograph.
- The US government photographs are public domain, but they must not be used to imply endorsement by the National Park Service or the USDA.
